I think that’s exactly it!  
Working isn’t getting rid of boredom first.

Working isn’t coaching yourself for half a day until you feel motivated. 
Working is feeling boredom, anxiety, doubt, uncertainty, and writing anyway.

You can notice it and decide not to participate.

“I notice I’m thinking it’s not working.”

“I notice I’m thinking what’s the point.”

And then continue. 
No fighting with your brain today. 
Just noticing and continuing and not struggling.

“I am capable of taking small steps that add up.”

“I notice I’m thinking it’s not working, and that’s okay.”

And when you figure that out, you’ll be able to help your clients do the exact same thing when their brain tells them, “It’s not working” and “What’s the point?”

Keep poking holes in the disbelief. 
And keep going coach!
