## I need help sorting through self doubt

**Date:** 2025-06-18  
**Topics:** self doubt

**QUESTION:**

There was a 2k for 2k Facebook community post by a member that talked about not being certified and struggling with feeling lost with knowing “how to coach”. I replied stating that I am in the exact same place. I have joined a certification program but it doesn’t “teach” anything. We have live calls each week where we practice, slowing down, leaving a lot of silence, asking questions that I find arbitrary or even nonsensical. I see the value in deep listening, holding space, slowing the conversation down and asking deep thought provoking questions. I have been struggling with whether or not to ask for a refund. Another member replied that there is a plan and roadmap and curriculum. I have been thinking about it ever since.

My thoughts: I am doing this all wrong. I have no business being a coach. If I coach someone, I am going to screw it all up and make a fool of myself. There is an educational path somewhere and I need to find it. My feelings: sad. defeated. hopeless. My actions: Ruminating, watching tv instead, try to convince myself that I can be a coach without this magical “plan, roadmap and curriculum”, do the dishes, avoid finding networking events, avoid sharing any of my experience and knowledge on social media, avoid sending any coaching session invitations like my coaching certification program encourages, search for another program that might finally be the one I have been looking for (the one that educates me on coaching). My results: No coaching clients.

I am a member of the Feminist Self Help Society with Kara Loewentheil. I am a member here and I am a member of the Ultimate Coaching program with the Insight Coaching Community.

Ultimately, I want to know what it means to be a great coach who can help people transform their lives after one coaching call. I am severely lacking the confidence that I have the knowledge to do that. I know Brooke Castillo will be offering some sort of training in the next few months and I desperately want to take part. I have no more money to invest in my coaching business, or me, until I get some clients.

Please help me sort through my thoughts and self doubt and create a plan. I need to know what baby steps to take each day to build my dream life. I am ready to face anything that comes up along the way to do that.

**ANSWER:**

You don’t feel confident coaching yet.

That’s where EVERYONE starts.

Or where they should start anyway.

It’s new, so of course you aren’t going to be great at it right away.

Think about anything else in life you’ve learned. Maybe it was a new job where you started out not knowing things and then learned and got good. Maybe it was in a sport. Or being a parent. Or learning how to drive a car for the first time.

You know you want to be a great coach and you’d like to come up with a plan for how to do that. You are already taking action and learning. You have learned more now than you knew a month ago.

With the training you are in, could you shift how you show up to learn more? For example, if you question why you are doing some of the things – do you feel like you could ask the instructors about it? It’s okay if you don’t feel like you could – but let’s explore how you might show up to get the most out of what you’ve already signed up for. That’s one step.

Another step is practicing coaching. It’s letting yourself be new and doing some coaching to learn what you know and where your gaps are now. Have you done some free coaching? How do you feel about doing some free coaching to get in some practice?

This coaching is meant to shift your mindset a bit – to help you see that where you are is normal and part of your journey. And to help your mind start to come up with a bit of a plan. Bring back your thoughts and what’s coming up for you reading this.
