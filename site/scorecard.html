AI Opportunity Scorecard

A department-by-department assessment to find where AI can help your team work smarter.

How to use this scorecard

This worksheet helps you assess where AI can reduce busywork, speed up decisions, and free up your people to focus on what only they can do.

For each department or function in your organization, you'll rate current pain on three dimensions: time spent on repetitive work, inconsistency across team members, and bottlenecks that slow decision-making.

Then you'll score your readiness to implement AI: data quality, process clarity, and team comfort with new tools.

The result is a priority map: which departments should tackle AI first, and what success in the first 90 days looks like.

Assessing Your Readiness to Implement AI

Once you've scored your pain points, assess your readiness in three areas:

  • Data quality: Can the AI tool access the data it needs? Is it clean and consistent?
  • Process clarity: Do you have documented processes to teach the AI how to help?
  • Team readiness: Is your team open to new tools? Do they have bandwidth to learn?

For each dimension, score:

Readiness DimensionScore 0Score 1Score 2
Data Quality Scattered, incomplete Mostly good, some gaps Clean and accessible
Process Clarity Processes aren't written Some documentation exists Clear, up-to-date playbooks
Team Readiness Skeptical or overloaded Willing but cautious Curious and ready to learn

Interpreting Your Score

Department Score: 13–18 points

This is your highest-impact starting point. There's significant pain, and the team is ready. Implement AI here first — you'll see the fastest ROI and build momentum for other departments.

Department Score: 7–12 points

Real opportunity, but either the pain is moderate or readiness needs work. Tackle this second. Use success from your first department to build team confidence.

Department Score: Below 7 points

Low priority right now. Either the problem isn't urgent, or the team isn't ready. Revisit after you've built momentum elsewhere.

Readiness Score: 5–6 points

You're ready to go. Your data is solid, processes are clear, and the team is willing. Move fast.

Readiness Score: 3–4 points

You can implement, but expect friction. Spend 2–3 weeks documenting processes and getting team buy-in first.

Readiness Score: Below 3 points

Pause implementation. Use the next month to clean up data, document processes, and talk to your team about why this matters. AI is only as good as the information you give it.
