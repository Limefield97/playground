About

Why me and not a consultant with a deck?

I spent 5 years writing TV for Disney and 20 years doing improv and live facilitation. Then I landed on a two-person marketing team at a food bank serving 23 parishes in South Louisiana — a workload that should require ten people.

AI is how we survive it. Over two years I've built the systems that write our talking points, monitor our media, produce our social content, and brief our leadership every morning. That work grew into helping our CEO plan operations across a 150+ person org and a seat on a leadership-sponsored AI initiative.

So when I train your team, you're not getting theory. You're getting the systems of someone who has to be right, on deadline, with no backup. And thanks to two decades on stage — the training is actually fun. People stay awake. On purpose.
