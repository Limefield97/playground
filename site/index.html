The moment everything changed

A writers' room, a story, and ten seconds.

I spent years as a TV writer living and working in Hollywood, including five years writing for Disney. In early 2023, weeks after ChatGPT launched, I gave it a premise our writers' room had spent an entire day cracking.

It came back in about ten seconds. With the same story.

A room full of professional writers, a full day of work — and the machine landed on the exact same answer before I finished my coffee.

That was the moment I saw what this technology actually was. Not a gimmick, and not a replacement for human creativity — I don't believe it should ever be that. But a tool that could take on an enormous amount of the work around the creativity, if someone learned it deeply enough to use it well.

So I did. I've used AI every working day since January 2023 — through my move from Hollywood to New Orleans, and into my current role as a Director of Communications and AI Strategy, where I build AI workflows, run trainings, and lead adoption across an entire organization.

Now I help New Orleans teams do the same: figure out where AI genuinely helps, learn it on their own work, and skip the two years of trial and error.
