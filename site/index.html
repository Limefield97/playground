01 · The story

I'm half of a two-person marketing team at a food bank that serves 23 parishes in South Louisiana. Press releases, social media, event marketing, media monitoring. All of it lands on the two of us.

Two years ago I got tired of working like the building was on fire, and I started building AI workflows to get my time back. They worked. Well enough that I ended up writing an AI opportunity assessment for every department in our 150-person organization, and our CEO put me on the initiative bringing AI into the whole operation.

Meanwhile, every leader I talked to said some version of the same sentence: "I know AI could help us. I just don't know where to start."

So now I show them. That's the business.
