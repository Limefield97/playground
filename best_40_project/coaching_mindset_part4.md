## Practical ways to make new intentional thoughts sink in andf stick…

**Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Topics:** intentional

**QUESTION:**

I know I have work to do as far as choosing new thoughts around my offer and how I can help people. How do you all get the new beliefs to stick? I know the subconscious is so tricky so I’m wondering what impact new thoughts will have if I consciously just think them? Do you write them? Say them? hang them up? i guess – how do we get them to go from thought/idea phase to new identities and rewiring?

Thank you!!

**ANSWER:**

Hi Coach!

Great question! While our subconscious is powerful, so it our conscious mind. The conscious mind is where we create those new thoughts we want to believe and the practice is really rehearsing those thoughts so often (by thought, journaling, saying it out loud, finding evidence to support them) that over time it moves into our subconscious mind – kind of on autopilot.

There are some teachings about something called the 95% vs. 5% in regards to our brain.

95% of our brain operates on autopilot…It consists of the thoughts, beliefs, patterns and habits we’ve practiced the most.

5% of our brain operates consciously and is the part we must use to create new thoughts, beliefs, patterns and habits.

The reason it feels hard for the new thoughts/patterns/habits to “stick” is because we have 95% of our brain power holding onto the older (more rehearsed) patterns/thoughts. It doesn’t however mean it’s impossible to change, it just means we have to invite our conscious brain (the 5%) to practice what we want MORE. Eventually those new thoughts, habits, beliefs and patterns become apart of our 95% programming and works in our favor.

Writing them, saying them, posting notes to keep seeing them, and finding evidence that supports those new thoughts are all great ways to make them more of a reality. Also connecting those new thoughts 
with emotions that feel good
 
in our body. When we do this, it’s kind of like creating a magnetic charge.

For example, “I can make 2k today”. That’s a thought that maybe doesn’t feel totally believable right now. But that’s only because 95% of the brain is finding evidence for the opposite. But, if we want to make 2k today, we need to keep that thought at the forefront and practice it often. Then we need to anchor into
 how we would actually feel
 if 2k came into our bank account every day. The feeling may be 
“elated, excited, thrilled”.

We want to 
hold those emotions with the thought,
 truly practicing
 the feeling of it in our body.

Over time, our brain can’t tell the difference between our thought/feeling being real or a dream… And when it gets to that place, we tend to create the result more often without having to consciously think it. Our subconscious begins to do more of the work for us.

1. What are some thoughts you want to practice thinking?

2. And what emotions are generated when you think them?

Feel free to come back with any thoughts or coaching you may still need! Thanks coach!
