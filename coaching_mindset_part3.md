based on

 invite in all walks of life
 and

But right now, your job isn’t to perfect the title. It’s to move your 1-year offer forward confidently and knowing internally what you’re capable of providing each client. You can invite people in, use the techniques you love, and deliver powerful coaching without needing to turn it into a 
perfectly defined brand/title
 just yet.

You know? What are your thoughts?
